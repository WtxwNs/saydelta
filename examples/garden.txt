On Saturday morning, the neighbors gathered beside the old railway station to begin a community garden. Maya brought a box of wooden labels, while Daniel arrived with a borrowed wheelbarrow and three shovels. The first job was to clear the stones from the narrow strip of land beside the fence. They agreed to leave the wild flowers near the gate because bees were already visiting them. Each household would look after one small bed and help water the shared fruit trees during the summer. Before planting anything, they tested the soil and marked a wide path for visitors who use wheelchairs. By lunchtime, the empty patch had become a place where people stopped to talk and exchange ideas. Nobody expected a harvest that day, but everyone went home with a reason to come back the following week.
